What Is a Soundboard Maker?

A soundboard is a grid of buttons that each play a different sound clip instantly when tapped. Soundboard makers let you create your own custom soundboards — uploading audio files, recording clips from your microphone, and arranging sounds on a grid. They are used for gaming (trigger sound effects during Discord calls or live streams), podcasting (play intro music, transition sounds, and audience reactions), presentations (quiz buzzers, applause, attention-grabbers), and fun (meme sounds, funny catchphrases, pranks). SoundTools Soundboard Maker runs entirely in your browser — no app to install, no account to create. Everything is private and local to your device.

How to Create a Custom Soundboard — Step by Step

Creating a soundboard with SoundTools is fast:

  • Upload or Record: Click "Upload Audio" to add any audio file (MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, AAC, M4A). Or click "Record" to capture a clip from your microphone. You can add as many sounds as you want.
  • Slice with the Waveform Editor: After uploading or recording, a waveform editor appears showing the full audio. Drag the start and end handles to select just the clip you want — a punchline, a sound effect, a short phrase. Click "Preview" to hear your selection on loop, then adjust until it's perfect.
  • Name and Color Your Buttons: Give each sound a descriptive name like "Air Horn" or "Sad Trombone." Pick a color to organize your board. Your soundboard auto-saves in your browser, so it's there when you come back.
  • Tap to Play: That's it — tap any button to trigger the sound instantly. Multiple sounds can play at the same time. The soundboard works on desktop and mobile, so you can use it on your phone during a call or presentation.

Soundboard Tips and Tricks

Keep clips short — 0.5 to 5 seconds is the sweet spot for instant-impact sound effects. Longer clips work for music beds and ambient sounds, but short punchy clips are what make a soundboard fun to use. Give each button a descriptive name so you can find sounds fast under pressure (during a live stream or call, you don't want to hunt for the right button). Use color coding to organize your board: red for fails and reactions, blue for music and stingers, green for positive sounds like applause and fanfare. Export clips and run them through our Voice Changer to create unique variants — a pitch-shifted air horn or a reverb-drenched rimshot can become your signature sound.

Is this a Voicemod soundboard alternative?

It depends what you need. Voicemod is a Windows desktop app with a soundboard feature designed specifically to route sounds into your microphone feed — other people in Discord or a game call hear the sound effects as if they're coming from your mic. SoundTools doesn't do virtual microphone routing. What it does instead: runs in your browser on any device (including iPhone and Android), lets you build a custom soundboard with uploaded or recorded clips, includes a waveform editor for precise slicing, and is completely free with no account required. If you need sounds to play through your mic in Discord, Voicemod is the right tool. Is this a Resanance alternative?

Similar answer to Voicemod. Resanance is a free Windows app that routes soundboard audio through a virtual microphone so others hear it in voice chat. SoundTools doesn't do virtual microphone audio routing — it plays sounds through your speakers or headphones, not into your mic feed.
